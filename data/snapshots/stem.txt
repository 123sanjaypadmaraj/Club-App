About Us
Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers.
The STEM Club at New Horizon College of Engineering is more than just a co-curricular group — it’s a creative hub where innovation meets exploration. It’s the place where science, technology, engineering, and mathematics merge to bring ideas to life.
Vision
To cultivate a vibrant community for aspiring engineers and
scientists, the STEM club envisions a dynamic space where
creativity, innovation, and collaboration thrive.
What We Do
We host workshops, hackathons, and challenge events that push you beyond textbooks and into real-world application.
We organise idea-blitz competitions, coding games, and maker projects that let you experiment with hardware, software and emerging technologies.
We bring the community together through mega-events like “Synapse 25’”, and smaller signature formats such as “Builder’s Asylum”, “Traffic 360”, and “E-Wasted”. (See past events for inspiration)
Benefits
Learn by doing: Theory is important — but real learning happens when you build, break, rebuild and innovate.
Network & collaborate: Meet like-minded peers, form project teams and get access to faculty guidance.
Showcase your talent: Whether it’s a coding challenge, design competition or prototype build-out, there are plenty of opportunities to shine.
Career-ready skills: Problem-solving, teamwork, prototyping, tech fluency — the gaps between academic concepts and industry needs are bridged here.
Our Mission
Encourage hands-on learning beyond classrooms through projects, workshops, and competitions.
Build a collaborative space where students from all disciplines can share ideas and grow together.
Empower members with the skills and confidence to lead in research, entrepreneurship, and innovation.
