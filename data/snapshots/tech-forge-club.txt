// 01 — WHO WE ARE
Forged in Innovation
Tech Forge Club is the co-curricular technical club of
New Horizon College of Engineering — a foundry for future engineers.
We take curiosity, crank up the heat, and shape it into skills that ship.
From electrifying tech fests like TECHFORGE '26 to heartfelt
outreach programs like Helping Hands and A Day of Giving, we live by
one motto — Driven by Tech »

// 05 — THE TIMELINE
The Forge Journey
EST. 2022
First Spark
Tech Forge Club is born at NHCE — a handful of students, one shared obsession: building cool things with technology.
MAY 2023
A Day of Giving
The club takes its energy off-campus to Sparsha Trust, Mathikere — tech kids proving that giving is also a skill.
MAY 2024
Helping Hands
Our outreach program scales up — more volunteers, more smiles, more impact in the community.
2024 → 2025
Forging Ahead
Workshops, seminars and guest lectures become a rhythm. Members battle it out at Kratos '24 and inter-college fests.
2026
TECHFORGE '26
The flagship fest returns bigger, louder and smarter — Code. Innovate. Automate. The forge burns hotter than ever.
