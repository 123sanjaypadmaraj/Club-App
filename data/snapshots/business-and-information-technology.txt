BIT Club Objectives
Educational Workshops and Seminars
Organize educational workshops, seminars, and guest speaker sessions to provide insights into technological advancements in the business world.
Hackathons and Tech Challenges
Conduct hackathons, coding competitions, and tech challenges to encourage hands-on learning, collaboration, and creative problem-solving.
Skill Development Workshops
Offer skill-building workshops on Coding, Data Analysis, Digital Marketing, Design Thinking, and Project Management to enhance students’ technical and professional abilities.
Tech Talks and Webinars
Arrange tech talks and webinars on specific technology-related topics delivered by professionals from both business and technology sectors.
Networking Events
Arranging networking sessions that bring together students, faculty, alumni and professionals from the business and technology sectors, facilitating valuable connections and mentorship opportunities.
